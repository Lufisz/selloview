Before you connect.

What about privacy?

The board shows store figures and order numbers. Customer names, emails and delivery addresses are not requested or shown.

Is the connection really read-only?

Yes. MetricTV reads the order and stock data it displays. It cannot edit your orders, products or store settings.

What do I need on the TV?

A TV or connected screen with a modern web browser. Open the pairing page on the TV and approve its code from your phone or laptop.

How often does the board update?

The TV checks for updates every 10 seconds. Shopify figures refresh about every 2 minutes. If the connection drops, the last figures stay visible with a warning.

What does the beta include?

The proposed free scope is one store, one screen. Access is currently limited to the pilot; other stores cannot connect yet. Period and light or dark appearance are configurable.
